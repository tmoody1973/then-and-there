# 005 — The Midwest is the home region, anchored on Milwaukee, Chicago and Detroit

**Decision.** The processed corpus and the demo focus on the Midwest. Explore anchors on Milwaukee, Chicago and Detroit, with Milwaukee as the deepest set. The game's "meanwhile, back home" falls back from city to state to the Midwest.

**Why this came up.** The plan started Milwaukee-only. Checking the archive showed **zero Milwaukee pages** for the dates of Bly's trip, so a Milwaukee-only "back home" couldn't work for the game.

**Options.**
1. **Milwaukee only.** Deepest local story; breaks the game's "back home" feature for 1889.
2. **The Midwest, with three anchor cities.** 11,499 Midwest pages exist for the trip dates. Chicago (34,275 front pages, 1880–1930) and Detroit (7,522) add Czech, Polish, Lithuanian, German and Black press.
3. **National, no focus.** Most coverage; loses the "my world" story the theme asks for.

**What we chose and why.** Option 2. Tarik asked for Chicago, Illinois, Detroit and Michigan; Claude checked the coverage. Three of the six papers that covered Bly most (St. Paul, Wichita, Indianapolis) are Midwestern.

**What we gave up.** Depth per city, within a $150 credit budget. Some precision: in 1889 the "back home" story will often come from a small-town paper, not the user's own city, and the card has to say so.

**How we'll know if this was right.** Every built game day has a Midwest "meanwhile" story that passes the Editor, and Explore returns strong results for each anchor city's community press.
