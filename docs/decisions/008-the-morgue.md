# 008 — Add "The Morgue": something to look up before every call

**Decision.** Each game day gets a free "Check the morgue" drawer: 2–3 real clippings printed on or before that day, about the place she's heading, her connections, or her published plans. Players can consult them before making the call. Only City Editor hints cost days.

**Why this came up.** Tarik asked whether the game teaches the way Carmen Sandiego did. Research showed Carmen's teaching engine was a reference book. The 1985 game shipped with *The World Almanac*, and players cross-referenced witness clues against it to choose the next country (Wikipedia; Computer History Museum: "we didn't really recognize that we were learning geography… or discovering research skills"). Our game had nothing to look up: you read one clipping and guessed. Against the Library of Congress's historical-thinking habits (Wineburg), we covered close reading and contextualizing, but not using background knowledge or corroborating before the call.

**Options.**
1. **Add the morgue now** (about half a day). Research becomes part of every call.
2. **Roadmap only.** Saves time for polish; the game stays "read one clipping, infer."
3. **Make the morgue the core.** Every call must be researched (closest to Carmen); 1–2 days and every day rewritten.

**What we chose and why.** Option 1, chosen by Tarik. It closes the biggest teaching gap at small cost, gives the Impact score (30%) a line teachers recognize, and connects the game to the Explore door, since the morgue is a Story Finder in miniature. "The morgue" is the newsroom's real name for its clippings library, so it fits the Copy Desk design.

**What we gave up.** Half a day. True *place* learning stays thin: most 1889 clippings about her ports are race coverage, so the morgue mostly teaches corroboration and research rather than what Colombo was like. 1889 descriptions of colonial ports can be racist or imperial, so v1 excludes flagged clippings from the morgue (a "whose voice is missing?" lesson is roadmap).

**How we'll know if this was right.** In play-testing, players open the morgue on most days before filing, and their correct-call rate is higher on days they opened it.
