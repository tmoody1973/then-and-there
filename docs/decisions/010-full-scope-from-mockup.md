# 010 — Build the full scope from Tarik's mockup

**Decision.** Build all six modes from Tarik's mockup (Today, Front Page, Detective, One Day Many Papers, Quests, Spot the Forgery, plus My Agent) in the mockup's visual style, instead of the two-door product (Play + Explore) the Oct 28 spec settled on.

**Why this came up.** On Oct 29, with Plan 3 shipped, Tarik looked at the live site next to his own 11-page mockup and judged the narrowed product too weak to win. The Oct 28 spec, shaped by a scope-skeptical pressure test (Claude's), had moved four of six modes to "roadmap only." Demo counts for 25% of judging, and a judge remembers a product with several distinct ways in better than one serial game and a search box.

**Options.**
1. *Keep the two-door product and polish it.* Lowest risk to the deadline. Cost: the product Tarik believes can't win.
2. *Build the mockup's full scope, the engine reused.* Every new mode sits on the existing page engine (cut, repair, classify, Editor check), so most of the work is screens and content. Cost: six days of build with little slack; the demo video and submission squeeze into the last day.
3. *Add one or two mockup modes only.* A middle path. Cost: still not the product Tarik designed.

**What we chose and why.** Option 2, Tarik's call. Build order (also Tarik's): the new look and Today first, then Front Page (Fri), Detective re-skin (Sat), One Day (Sun), Spot the Forgery (Mon, after a one-hour Muse Image test), Quests (Tue), submission (Wed). The guide keeps the name "the City Editor"; the mockup's "The Chief" is Carmen Sandiego's boss and the spec bans that echo. Explore stays, reached by a small "Search the archive" link.

**What we gave up.** Slack. If a day slips, a mode ships smaller (fewer quest chapters, fewer forgery rounds), not later. The finish design review and DESIGN.md wait until all screens exist, rather than running per screen.

**How we'll know if this was right.** By Wed Nov 4, 9 PM ET: every mode in the nav opens a playable screen built from real, cited pages on the live site, on a phone and a laptop, and the demo shows at least four modes in three minutes.
