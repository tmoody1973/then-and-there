# 012 — The Front Page year slider ends at 1930, not 1963

**Decision.** The "When?" slider on Front Page runs from 1770 to 1930. The mockup (pages 2 and 9) drew it from 1770 to 1963.

**Why this came up.** The Nov 1 design review noticed that every page in the Front Page rotation was printed before 1931. That limit is a fixed rule of the project: Chronicling America's pages are public domain only up to 1930. With the slider running to 1963, about a sixth of the track was years that can never be the answer. A careful player would waste guesses there, and a judge might read the range as a promise of later pages.

**Options.**
1. *Keep 1963, as drawn.* Matches the mockup exactly. Cost: a dead stretch of slider, and a hint of content that doesn't exist.
2. *End at 1930.* The slider covers only years a page could come from. Cost: it departs from the approved mockup, and a player now knows no page is later than 1930, which makes the game a little easier.
3. *Narrow both ends to the pages we actually hold.* Most precise. Cost: the range would change whenever the rotation changes, and it gives away even more.

**What we chose and why.** Option 2. Claude made the call while fixing the review, under Tarik's "fix everything in the report." The 1930 limit is already public: it's in the product's own rules and on the Explore page ("all printed before 1931").

**What we gave up.** Fidelity to the mockup on one number, and a little difficulty.

**How we'll know if this was right.** In play-tests, guesses cluster inside the real range, and nobody asks why the slider stops at 1930.
