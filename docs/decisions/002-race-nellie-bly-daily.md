# 002 — The game is a daily serial: race Nellie Bly in real time, asking "will she make it?"

**Decision.** The one game is a chase built from real 1889–90 newspapers. One dispatch unlocks per day on its 137th anniversary (starting Nov 14, 2026), and each day the player answers a question 1889 readers actually faced about whether Bly would make her connections.

**Why this came up.** Tarik wanted a game in the spirit of "Where in the World Is Carmen Sandiego," plus a reason for people to come back daily. The earlier plan had two games (a daily front-page guessing game and a detective case), which would split a 3-minute demo and a 7-day build.

**Options.**
1. **Two games** (daily front-page game + Bly case). Two habits, but neither gets the spotlight, and the front-page game reads as "TimeGuessr for newspapers."
2. **One game, played daily**: the Bly chase as a serial. One habit, one memorable demo, launches on a real anniversary.
3. **Daily front-page game only.** Easiest to build, least original.

**What we chose and why.** Option 2. Tarik chose the chase and the "will she make it?" question; Claude proposed the daily serial format and the real-time anniversary. Coverage supports it: 64 of the 73 trip days have at least one archive page mentioning her. The *Evening World* (Nov 29, 1889) printed the real contest to guess her exact time, which becomes the finale. "Will she make it?" replaced "where next?" after an independent review found the first week is all at sea, and her route was printed in advance.

**What we gave up.** The daily front-page game (now roadmap). A pure route-guessing mechanic. Launching the full 72 days before judging: only ~6 dramatic days ship by Nov 4.

**How we'll know if this was right.** Test players come back on day 2 without a reminder, and judges remember the "race her in real time, 137 years later" line after the demo.
