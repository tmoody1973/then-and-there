# Surface brief: Play ("Race Nellie Bly")

Scope: /play, /play/[day], /play/finale, /how. Visitor mode: Operate (a short daily task), with immersion as the payoff.
Audience and job: a phone player with two minutes; read one real 1889 dispatch, make a "will she make it?" call, see the reveal. Judges play in demo mode.
Takeaway (Tarik): "I was there in 1889." Anti-goals (Tarik): generic quiz app; fake-vintage costume (sepia, parchment, ye-olde type); museum-dull; cluttered at 375 px. Loves: telegraph/wire forms, railway and steamship timetables, modern newspaper apps.
Memorable moment: the scan arrives on the desk with the clipping ringed in blue pencil.

## Direction contract

THESIS: You are working the 1889 city desk. The real page is the proof in front of you; the editor's blue pencil is the only color and the only voice of judgment. Refuses the category default of quiz cards, confetti, and the cream-serif-red "vintage editorial" look.

OWN-WORLD: Cool newsprint white ground (never cream or sepia, no textures), true ink black text, gray rule lines like column rules. One accent: copy-desk blue pencil, used only for marks that mean something (Bly's route, the player's circled call, the City Editor's margin notes, focus rings). Controls are proofreader's marks: circle to choose, caret for an inserted hint, "stet" to keep, strike for a missed call. Headlines in a Franklin-style newspaper grotesque; reading text in a sturdy book serif (Charter stack). Scans shown flat and unfiltered.

STORY: The player sees the actual page, reads what 1889 readers read, marks a call in pencil, optionally asks the City Editor (each ask costs a day), then sees the next day's real clipping prove it. They leave believing the archive is alive and the pages are real.

FIRST VIEWPORT: At 375 px: slim masthead line (THEN & THERE · DAY N · weekday month day, 1889), days-left and streak on one rule-separated line; the scan crop as the dominant element (about 55% of the viewport height), the clipping ringed in blue pencil, citation set as a caption under it; the clean transcription begins just below the fold edge. The call sits directly after the transcription, so it's one thumb scroll away. Primary action: circling a choice, then "File it."

FORM: Copy Desk, position 4 on the ordered list of seven; seed key 2172cf11. Fused from challengers: the route rides over everything in one ink (orienteering map); connection states made / missed / delayed as explicit states (transit diagram). Signature interaction: the blue-pencil ring draws itself around the clipping when the dispatch loads, and around the player's choice when they file, one stroke, under 400 ms, respecting reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
